Platform design

ETL, a reconciliation builder, a dashboard builder, models for insights and anomaly detection, and a notification engine. I’ve also applied these patterns in the design of multiple internal management tools.

What I design

I start with the financial event, the system that records it and the control that proves it was handled correctly. I then decide what belongs in the platform and what teams should be able to configure for each process.

  1. Ingest and validate
  2. Match and explain
  3. Review and approve
  4. Confirm posting
  5. Monitor and alert
ETL builder
I define source adapters and configurable mappings for files, APIs and tables. Processing rules cover fixed-width and delimited layouts, record types, business dates, currency precision and debit/credit signs. I design validation, rejected-record handling and batch controls so operators can account for what entered the pipeline and safely rerun it.
Reconciliation builder
I design how teams configure source priority, match keys, date windows, tolerances and one-to-one or grouped matching. The design keeps candidate selection, the match decision and exception handling distinct, so an operator can inspect the rule and records behind a result.
Dashboard builder
I design configurable dimensions, measures and filters for match rates, open value, ageing and intake health. Each summary needs a path back to the records it counts, with clear definitions for denominators, business dates and currencies.
Models for insights and anomaly detection
I design signals for unusual break volumes, amount shifts, late files and changes in recurring mismatch patterns. I compare signals with the usual range for the source and cycle so expected volume changes do not dominate the review queue. Models help prioritize investigation. Matching and accounting decisions follow the configured rules and approval controls.
Notification engine
I design event triggers, recipients and escalation rules around operational events such as missing inputs, failed runs, pending approvals and ageing exceptions. In-app and email notifications should point to the affected run or work item so the recipient has enough context to act.
Internal management tools
I design operational workspaces around file readiness, run status, work queues and maker-checker approval. Operators need to know what is pending, who owns it and what changed. Reviewers need the evidence behind a rerun, override or posting decision.

Card reconciliation, from clearing to ledger

Visa is the worked example. I check that clearing records are complete, tie the resulting position to settlement, then confirm what reached the ledger. Mastercard and bank reporting feeds require different comparisons, shown alongside it.
